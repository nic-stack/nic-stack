## 🔭 Featured Projects
Real public data, with every number checked and reproducible.

| Project | Question and answer |
|---------|---------------------|
| [**Target Margin Analysis**](https://github.com/nic-stack/target-margin-analysis) | Why did Target's operating income fall 57% in fiscal 2022 while revenue grew? SEC filings for Target, Walmart and Kohl's show the cost-of-goods rate explains $5.06B of the $5.10B drop. Ends in a one-page CEO brief |
| [**Household Financial Strain**](https://github.com/nic-stack/household-financial-strain-2023) | Which U.S. households are stretched by housing costs? 2023 BLS survey data, survey-weighted with 95% intervals and cross-checked in SQL and pandas: 32.1% of households (42.2M), and 53.5% of renters |
| [**Medicare Payment Variation**](https://github.com/nic-stack/medicare-payment-variation) | Where does Medicare pay more for the same procedure? A dbt + DuckDB pipeline on 9.78M rows of 2024 CMS data with 19 data tests and a [live Tableau dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/medicare_dashboard/Dashboard): visit gaps are mostly local cost, imaging gaps largely reflect what was billed |
| [**Where Airline Delay Starts**](https://github.com/nic-stack/flight-delay-knock-on) | How much flight delay is the airline's own? Following knock-on delay plane by plane through 7.06M U.S. flights (BTS) in SQL and Python: about 45% starts with the airline (33% when each flight is counted on its own), and morning delays spread most. Tableau dashboard |
| [**Social Media Performance**](https://github.com/nic-stack/social-media-analysis) | What works on Instagram, LinkedIn and Facebook? 1,053 posts from accounts I managed, with matched-post statistical tests and an [interactive dashboard](https://nic-stack.github.io/social-media-analysis/) |
